The Evolution of Human Communication

MISSION BRIEFING

Every technological shift was a revolution in communication. Writing allowed laws to outlive authors. Printing made dissent scalable. Electricity annihilated distance. Today, AI speaks every language at once.

WRITING TECHNOLOGY

  • Cuneiform: Durable data storage on wet clay.
  • Papyrus: Portable, flexible information recording.
  • Paper (105 CE): Massively reduced the cost of literacy.
