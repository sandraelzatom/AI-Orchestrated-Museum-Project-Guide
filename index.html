Why Communication Changes Everything

Every technological revolution has been, at its core, a revolution in communication. Writing allowed laws to outlive authors. The printing press made dissent scalable. The electric telegraph annihilated distance. We move from clay tablet to quantum bit.

Era 01 // Smoke & Stone

The Literacy Shift

Cuneiform (3200 BCE): Sumerians used reed styluses on wet clay, creating the first durable data storage.

Paper (105 CE): Cai Lun drastically lowered the cost of information recording using hemp and rags.

Era 02 // Mechanical

Mass Production

Gutenberg (1440): Reusable metal type broke the monopoly of handwritten manuscripts.

Steam Press (1814): Output accelerated from hundreds to thousands of pages per hour.
